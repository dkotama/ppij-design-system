## Permission

Use of the PPIJ logo requires permission from **PPI Jepang** first. This
design system showing the logo is documentation, not a grant of permission.

## The one rule

The logo is shown **as it is**. The AD/ART (Anggaran Dasar dan Anggaran
Rumah Tangga) of PPIJ prescribes the logo, and the logo shall be displayed in
that prescribed form. Source: AD/ART PPIJ.

That means: never invert the logo, never recolor it, never outline it, never
rotate, stretch, crop, or add effects to it, and never place it on a
background where the prescribed colors cannot read as themselves. A dark
background version does not exist, because that would require an inverted
logo.

## Not permitted

- Inverted or white-only versions
- Recolored versions, including a PPIJ red only version
- The logo on dark or busy photo backgrounds
- Rotated, stretched, cropped, shadowed, or outlined versions
- The logo as a pattern, watermark, or decoration
- Use without permission from PPI Jepang
